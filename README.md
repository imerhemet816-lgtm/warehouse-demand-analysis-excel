# Warehouse Sales & Demand Analytics

## Project Overview
An Excel-based analytical project exploring warehouse transactions, outbound product movement, and demand patterns to support operational planning.

## Dataset
- 500 transactions
- 1,242 product movement records
- 10 products
- 3 warehouses

## Tools & Skills
Microsoft Excel, Data Analysis, Data Visualization, KPI Reporting, Product Affinity Analysis, Operations Analytics.

## Business Objectives
- Identify frequently co-occurring products.
- Analyze hourly and weekly outbound activity.
- Compare warehouse-level performance.
- Classify products by demand intensity.
- Identify operational and replenishment planning opportunities.

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Outbound Units: 6,784
- Highest Outbound Volume Product: Duz (775 units)
- Most Frequent Product Pair: Yağ + Çay (36 transactions)

## Workbook Structure
Overview, Product Analysis, Warehouse Analysis, Hourly Intensity, Weekly Demand, Weekday Demand, Frequent Pairs, Frequent Triples, Data Audit.

## Key Business Insights
- Certain products contribute more heavily to outbound volume.
- Product co-occurrence patterns can support warehouse picking and layout decisions.
- Hourly and weekday activity patterns can inform workload planning.

## Data Limitations
The dataset does not contain opening inventory, inbound receipts, or stock snapshots. Therefore, closing stock balances, actual inventory turnover, and overstock levels cannot be reliably calculated.

## Business Recommendations
- Review high-movement products for replenishment planning.
- Evaluate pick-slotting opportunities for frequently co-occurring products.
- Collect inventory balance information to enable turnover and stock analysis.
